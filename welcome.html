    Questions 🤔

    Why isn't it on the Play Store or App Store?

    It's a free hobby project, and the stores charge fees and take time. Chrome and Safari can install web apps (called PWAs) straight from the browser. You get a home-screen icon, full screen and offline use, just without the store.

    Can the developer see my spending?

    No. There is no server and no account. Your entries stay on your phone. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share something, like your card image.

    Does it read my SMS or connect to my bank?

    No. You type every spend yourself. That's the point: no access to anything, and you actually feel each rupee.

    What permissions does it need?

    None to start. Photos only if you choose to attach one. Notifications only if you turn them on.

    What if I change or lose my phone?

    Your data lives only on your phone, so use Settings → Save backup now and then. The app reminds you weekly.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No ads, no paid features. There's an optional "buy the dev a chai" tip inside. The app works the same either way.

    Is it financial advice?

    No. Scores and tips are for fun and general learning. For real money decisions, talk to a qualified professional.
