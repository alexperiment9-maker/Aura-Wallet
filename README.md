# Aura Wallet 🗿

A free, goofy, manual expense tracker that scores you on budgeting and, most of all, on giving.

- **Not connected to any bank.** You type every entry.
- **No server, no account.** Data stays on your device.
- **Installable** from the browser (Add to Home screen) and works offline.

## Features

- Log spends and gives in a few taps, with optional photos.
- Custom categories with daily, weekly or monthly budgets, plus overall limits.
- Scores: Give (main), Discipline, Consistency, Frugality, combined into Aura.
- A collectible-style stat card you can share as an image.
- Squad: friends swap codes and are ranked by the share of their money they give, not the amount.
- Badges, levels, streaks, daily reminder, weekly backup reminder.
- Advisor tab: money-health score, charts (day by day, by category, month by month, weekday), findings on where you overspend, and a cut plan. All worked out on the phone.
- Repeating spends (rent, subscriptions, EMIs) logged automatically each month.
- Monthly recap with a shareable story image (percentages only, no ₹ amounts).
- Four vibes: Goofy, Nonchalant, Pookie and Chad (with Stoic quotes). Jokes in English or Hinglish.
- Scores unlock after 3 logged days, so nobody gets free aura.
- Backup / restore (optionally password-encrypted) and CSV export.
- Brain food: daily money tip, quotes and a goofy dictionary of 50+ banking words (general education, not advice).

## Install

Start here: **https://alexperiment9-maker.github.io/Aura-Wallet/welcome.html**

- **Android:** open in Chrome → ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** → **Install**. If it says "Could not open app", choose **Create shortcut**.
- **iPhone:** open in Safari → **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**. Always open it from the Home Screen icon.

Opening the link inside WhatsApp or Instagram? Switch to Chrome or Safari first.

## Sharing

- **Invite & flex** (Card or Squad tab) makes a story-size image with your card and a QR code to the app.
- Links show a preview image in WhatsApp and other apps.

## Files

- `index.html`: the app. `welcome.html`: the landing page to share.
- `qr.js`: QR code library by Kazuhiko Arase (MIT licence), bundled so nothing loads from outside.
- `reminder.ics`: daily 9:30 PM calendar reminder for iPhone.
